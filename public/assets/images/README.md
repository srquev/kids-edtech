# Learning pictures

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then compressed to JPEG for local offline use. These are AI-generated realistic illustrations, not photographs of actual animals or fruit. The two atlases are displayed one cell at a time using `imageGrid` metadata, shared between English and Hindi and between lessons and games.

Assets: `animals-realistic.jpg` (4 × 4), `fruits-realistic.jpg` (5 × 2). Read cells left to right, top to bottom. Original PNGs remain in the generation library.

## Final prompts

### Animals

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: ONE square sprite atlas for a preschool learning app. Create a 4 by 4 grid of 16 photorealistic full-body animals on a plain warm white background, no text, no borders. Every cell is exactly the same size, aligned to a precise regular grid. Each animal fully contained in its own cell with generous 15% margins; no crossing cell edges. Natural anatomy, recognizable realistic textures, gentle calm poses, studio lighting, child-friendly. Row 1 left to right: elephant, male lion with mane, tiger, golden retriever dog. Row 2: domestic cat, rabbit, brown bear, giant panda. Row 3: monkey, red fox, black and white dairy cow, pink pig. Row 4: horse, woolly sheep, giraffe, zebra. Exactly one animal per cell. No accessories, no scenery, no cartoon faces, no labels. Square image, high resolution.

### Fruits

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: ONE landscape sprite atlas for a preschool learning app. Create a precise 5 columns by 2 rows regular grid of realistic fruit studio photographs, aspect ratio 5:2. Plain warm white background, no text, no borders. Cells square and identical. Every fruit fully inside its cell with generous 15% margins. Row 1 left to right: one red apple with leaf; one yellow banana; one bunch of purple grapes; one whole orange; one strawberry. Row 2 left to right: one green striped watermelon with a red cut wedge beside it; one whole pineapple with its leafy crown; one ripe golden orange mango; one green pear; a pair of red cherries on stems. Photorealistic textures and natural colors, soft lighting, crisp recognizable silhouettes, no faces, no decorations, no other objects. Each listed fruit occupies exactly one cell.
